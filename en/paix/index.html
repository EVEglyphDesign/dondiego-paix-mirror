Peace for the childrenPAIX · Education

PAIX (French for peace) is an early-childhood education platform that walks with mothers, children, and fathers. It is offered as an extension of the work that Fundación Comunitaria Don Diego A.C. already carries out in the Santa Teresita de Don Diego community, in the same spirit of accompaniment, care, and integral development that the Foundation already practises.

Three doors · one community

Each person enters through their own door. The conversation begins with who you are, not what you know how to do.

Mother

Mother's Door

Accompaniment tools, digital-safety guidance for childhood, and a listening line with the psychologists at the Educational Centre. The mother is the first educator.

Child

Child's Door

A space for play and learning — reading, art, music, motor skills, first steps with a computer. The Village lets children live together with voice and movement inside the school circle, without open social networks.

Father

Father's Door

Leadership workshops, trades, and a practical guide to how artificial intelligence enters the home. The father is not left out of the educational conversation.

How it plugs into the Foundation

The Foundation's programme already prioritises education, psychological support, and health, in that order. PAIX plugs into that same order without replacing anything that already works:

  • Education — reinforces the reading, art, computing, and piano the Educational Centre already offers, with bilingual digital resources (ES/EN/FR).
  • Psychological support — a private, consent-based channel for the mothers and young people already working with the Centre's psychologists.
  • Health — a safety-first, betterment-second guide to the use of screens, phones, and AI with children.

«Learning to read is crucial for the development of children at school and for their success beyond school.» — Fundación Comunitaria Don Diego A.C.

The Village · a shared space for the children

The Village is a shared space inside the PAIX educational console, after lesson time. Voice and movement are the primary form of interaction. The school circle — the Educational Centre itself — is the boundary. There is no global feed, no discovery between strangers, no follower metrics. A child's reputation grows through evidenced acts of service, not by screen presence.

PAIX educational game

A 3D action-adventure game, in development with Godot, designed to run inside the Educational Centre. It is a doorway, not a destination: education, accompaniment, and free choice are the process the game carries.

Consent and data

PAIX is consent-first. No mother or child enters a record without explicit consent. Any data a participant leaves in PAIX stays under their control, with the right to export and delete at any time. This principle sits behind everything else.
